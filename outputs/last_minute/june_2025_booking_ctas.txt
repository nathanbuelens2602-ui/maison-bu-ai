Philosophy behind every CTA:
The guest should feel invited, never targeted.
Quiet confidence, never urgency.
The feeling of a window opening — not a sale beginning.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CTA 01 — For Instagram Feed Posts (general June)
  Placement: Last line of caption
  Context: Any brand or pillar post
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"Link in bio to reserve your summer ritual."

— Why it works: "reserve" implies value and intention, not transaction.
  "summer ritual" elevates the service into an experience.
  It does not say "book" or "appointment." It says this is yours to claim.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CTA 02 — For Instagram Stories (mid-June)
  Placement: Slide 3 of a Story flow
  Context: After showing the craft or a transformation
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"The atelier is ready to receive you.
 A few moments remain in June."

[Followed by booking link sticker]

— Why it works: "receive" is the Maison BU word — it implies
  hospitality, not commerce. "A few moments remain" creates
  real scarcity without countdown clocks or caps lock.
  The link appears after a breath — the invitation is felt, then offered.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CTA 03 — For Email (end of campaign email body)
  Placement: Final paragraph before sign-off
  Context: Campaign or newsletter
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"A few June appointments remain. We would love to receive you
 before the month closes and the calendar fills for July."

[CTA button: "Reserve your summer ritual"]

— Why it works: No imperative, no urgency word, no promotional signal.
  "We would love to receive you" is a human statement. It cannot be
  faked or replicated by a brand that doesn't genuinely mean it.
  "Before the month closes" references natural time, not fake scarcity.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CTA 04 — For SMS / WhatsApp (last-minute availability)
  Character count: 158 (under 160)
  Context: When a specific slot opens
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"A quiet opening appeared in the atelier for this week —
 for a Signature Cut or beachwaves, tailor-made.
 We thought of you. maisonbu.be/book"

— Why it works: "appeared" makes it feel organic, not manufactured.
  "We thought of you" is the most powerful phrase in last-minute
  marketing — because it implies selection, not broadcast.
  The booking URL is bare, functional and unhurried at the end.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CTA 05 — For Instagram Story / Last-minute slide
  Placement: Standalone story, not part of a flow
  Context: Specific slot or slow week
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"One opening remains this week.
 Thursday — afternoon.
 The light in the atelier is particularly beautiful this time of year."

[Booking link sticker below]

— Why it works: Three short lines do three distinct things.
  Line 1: The fact — real scarcity, stated without drama.
  Line 2: The specificity — a day, a time. It becomes real.
  Line 3: The desire — why now? Because the atelier is beautiful.
  The booking link arrives after the desire is created, not before.
  The guest books because they want to. Not because they were pushed.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  UNIVERSAL CTA PRINCIPLES — MAISON BU
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

What every Maison BU CTA must do:
  ✓ Feel like an invitation, not a sales trigger
  ✓ Use natural language, not marketing language
  ✓ Reference real availability or a genuine moment — never fabricated
  ✓ Follow desire, not create fake urgency
  ✓ Allow the guest to feel they are choosing, not being persuaded

What no Maison BU CTA will ever do:
  ✗ Use the word "hurry," "now," "don't miss," "last chance"
  ✗ Use countdown timers or promotional framing
  ✗ Appear before the desire is created
  ✗ Use more than one CTA in a single piece of content
  ✗ Sound like it was written by a marketing department

The test: read it aloud as Kristof.
If it sounds like something a person would genuinely say —
it is a Maison BU CTA.
