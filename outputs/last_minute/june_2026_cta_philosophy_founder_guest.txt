THE MAISON BU CTA PRINCIPLE
  A call to action at Maison BU is never a call.
  It is an opening of a door — and a step back, to let the guest decide.
  The guest should feel they are choosing to enter.
  Not that they were pushed through.

THE FOUR-WEEK CTA EVOLUTION

  Week 1 — No CTA (or invisible)
  Goal: World-building only.
  The only CTA acceptable in Week 1: the wordless one.
  The beauty of the content itself. The desire it creates.
  If a link appears at all, it is at the absolute end —
  never the reason for the post.
  Maximum one per week. Zero is also correct.

  Week 2 — The Whisper
  Goal: First soft invitation. Desire created before the ask.
  CTA arrives only after at least 100 words of atmosphere.
  Language: quiet, personal, specific.
  Examples:
    "Link in bio to reserve your summer ritual."
    "Reserve your moment. Link in bio."
    "Summer appointments available. →"
  Never: "Book now." "Don't wait." "Limited spots."

  Week 3 — The Considerate Ask
  Goal: Event and ceremony bookings. Higher stakes, warmer tone.
  CTA is more direct here — the guest has a specific need (wedding,
  communion, event) and needs to feel guided, not pressured.
  Examples:
    "Bridal and event appointments: hello@maisonbu.be"
    "Communion appointments available in June. Link in bio."
    "Reserve your ceremony appointment — a few remain."
  Note: Email CTAs for ceremony content are more effective than
  link stickers. They imply a personal conversation, not a system.

  Week 4 — The Invitation
  Goal: Convert desire to booking. The campaign's only commercial week.
  CTAs are present on every piece — but always as final lines,
  always after atmosphere, always as invitation not urgency.
  Language: composed, warm, certain.
  Examples:
    "The atelier is ready. The calendar is nearly full.
     A window remains for those who know."
    "We would be glad to receive you. Link in bio."
    "A few moments remain in June. Reserve yours →"
    "July begins soon. Before it does — maisonbu.be/book"

THE MAISON BU CTA HIERARCHY
  Most powerful (use sparingly): "We thought of you."
  Second: "A window has opened."
  Third: "A few moments remain."
  Fourth: "Reserve your summer ritual."
  Fifth: "Link in bio."
  Least powerful (but still on-brand): "→ maisonbu.be/book"

  Use in reverse order through the month — save the most powerful
  for the moments when genuine urgency exists (Week 4, last-minute).

WHAT NO MAISON BU CTA WILL EVER SAY IN JUNE 2026
  "Summer sale" / "June offer" / "Don't miss out"
  "Book before it's too late" / "Limited spots — act now"
  "Get your summer glow" / "Treat yourself this summer"
  "Hair goals achieved" / "Summer transformation starts here"
  Any countdowns, any timers, any promotional framing whatsoever.

║   FOUNDER PHILOSOPHY CONTENT IDEAS — KRISTOF IN JUNE            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Philosophy: Kristof's voice in content is never promotional.
            It is the voice of someone who has spent years thinking
            deeply about craft, beauty, hospitality, and what it means
            to truly receive a person in a moment of transformation.
            It builds trust before it builds anything else.

Kristof content appears: Week 1 (Friday), Week 2 (optional mid-week),
                          Week 3 (optional ceremony context).
Format: Single image with caption in his voice, or Reel 05.

CONTENT IDEA 01 — "On Summer and Restraint"
  Theme: The craft of doing less
  Opening line: "In summer, the work becomes about ease.
                 Not simplicity — ease. There is a difference."
  Core idea: Mastery = knowing what to remove, not what to add.
             The most beautiful hair in summer requires more skill —
             precisely because it must appear effortless.
  Visual: Kristof at the window. Thinking. The city in summer light.
  Tone: Philosophical. Quiet. The confidence of someone who has
        thought about this for a long time.

CONTENT IDEA 02 — "On Listening Before Cutting"
  Theme: Consultation as the most important part
  Opening line: "We never begin with what we want to create."
  Core idea: Understanding the person is more important than the result.
             Hair that doesn't belong to the person doesn't belong anywhere.
  Visual: Kristof in consultation. His attention — visible and total.
  Tone: Grounded, human, direct. This is the Maison BU founding belief.

CONTENT IDEA 03 — "On June Specifically"
  Theme: What this month means to the atelier
  Opening line: "June is the month the atelier breathes differently."
  Core idea: The quality of June light — how it shapes the work,
             how it changes what guests want and what they receive.
             How each month has its own emotional register.
  Visual: Kristof + the June atelier. Light as subject.
  Tone: Warm, seasonal, personal.

CONTENT IDEA 04 — "On Ceremony"
  Theme: Week 3 — what Kristof believes about important days
  Opening line: "Some days ask everything of the people inside them."
  Core idea: On the responsibility of caring for someone's hair on
             a wedding morning or a communion day. The weight of that.
             And why Maison BU approaches it the same way as any
             other appointment — with the same total attention.
  Visual: Kristof at work during a ceremony preparation.
  Tone: Reverent, honest, moving. His most personal voice.

CONTENT IDEA 05 — "On the Guest Who Carries Invisible Pressure"
  Theme: Empathy for the person behind the appointment
  Opening line: "People arrive here carrying more than they show."
  Core idea: The Maison BU guest lives a demanding life.
             This appointment is, often, one of the few moments
             where absolutely nothing is asked of them.
             What Kristof understands about that weight —
             and why hospitality is not separate from craft.
  Visual: The atelier. A guest, barely seen. A quality of stillness.
  Tone: The most emotionally intelligent piece in the campaign.
        Not to be used lightly — when it lands, it builds years of loyalty.

CONTENT IDEA 06 — "On Not Chasing Trends"
  Theme: Why Maison BU's approach to fashion is selective
  Opening line: "We are aware of everything happening in hair.
                 We apply almost none of it."
  Core idea: Trends pass through the lens of: does this serve
             this specific person? Does it serve their life?
             Their face? Their future? If not — it is not applied.
             Timelessness over trend. Identity over moment.
  Visual: The scissors. The craft. Not a trend board.
  Tone: Clear, confident, slightly contrarian. Deeply on-brand.

║   GUEST EMOTION CONTENT IDEAS — JUNE 2026                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Philosophy: Guest emotion content captures the feeling of a Maison BU
            experience from the inside. Not testimonials. Not reviews.
            Emotional observations — told as stories, as moments,
            as truths the viewer will recognise about themselves.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 01 — "She Arrived Carrying a Tuesday"
  Theme: The relief of an appointment
  The story: A guest who arrived with the invisible weight of
             a difficult week. What happened during the appointment.
             What she left with.
  Opening: "She arrived carrying a Tuesday."
  Key emotion: Relief. Restoration. The feeling of being received.
  Format: Feed post caption (Post 05 — already written).
           Could also be a Reel 03 or 08 closing text.
  Note: "She" — never named, always specific. The viewer is her.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 02 — "The Guest Who Couldn't Explain What She Wanted"
  Theme: The consultation as emotional translation
  The story: She came in unable to articulate what she needed.
             Not technically — emotionally. She felt wrong in her hair
             but couldn't say why. The consultation as the moment
             someone finally heard her.
  Opening: "She knew it was wrong. She just didn't have the words."
  Key emotion: Being understood without having to explain.
  Format: Feed caption (The Transformation pillar) or Reel 05 variant.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 03 — "The Bride the Day After"
  Theme: What remains after the ceremony
  The story: A bride who messaged the atelier the day after her wedding.
             Not about the hair — about the feeling of having been cared for
             so carefully on the most important morning.
  Opening: "She sent a message the morning after."
  Key emotion: Gratitude, loyalty, the feeling of having been seen.
  Format: Stories (Week 3 close). Or a testimonial-adjacent caption —
           but written as narrative, never as a direct quote.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 04 — "The First Appointment in Months"
  Theme: Re-engagement, the feeling of returning
  The story: A guest who hadn't visited since autumn. What it felt like
             to come back. The recognition — her name remembered,
             her preferences known, the chair felt familiar.
  Opening: "She hadn't been in since October."
  Key emotion: Belonging. The loyalty that comes from being remembered.
  Format: A Week 4 feed post or re-engagement email.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 05 — "She Left It Until June"
  Theme: The guest who almost didn't come
  The story: She had been meaning to book since May. Something
             always came up. Then one Tuesday, a window appeared.
             She almost didn't take it. She did. What happened next.
  Opening: "She had been meaning to come since May."
  Key emotion: The quiet reward of finally doing the thing you kept
               putting off. Summer beginning at the right moment.
  Format: Week 4 Story Flow or caption — the perfect booking content
           that doesn't feel like booking content.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 06 — "The Summer She Decided to Stop Rushing"
  Theme: The guest as a whole person, not just a client
  The story: A guest who changed something about how she approaches
             summer this year. Slower. More intentional.
             The Maison BU appointment as part of that decision.
  Opening: "This summer, she decided to stop rushing."
  Key emotion: The alignment of Maison BU's values with the guest's
               own desire to slow down, to choose quality, to be present.
  Format: A reflective Week 1 or Week 4 piece. The most philosophical
           of the guest emotion series — and potentially the most powerful.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 07 — "Before the Vacation"
  Theme: Vacation preparation as ritual
  The story: Packing. Planning. The things a person does before
             leaving for somewhere warm. And the appointment at Maison BU —
             the final ritual before departure. Arriving at the airport
             feeling completely ready.
  Opening: "The flight is in three days."
  Key emotion: Preparation, excitement, the desire to arrive already beautiful.
  Format: Story Flow 15 is the companion to this — or a Week 2/3 feed post.

GUEST EMOTION IDEA 08 — "The Communion Morning"
  Theme: A parent watching a child's hair being cared for
  The story: A mother in the atelier with her child on communion morning.
             Not about the child's hair — about watching someone
             care for your child with the same attention they would
             give to any guest. The trust that creates.
  Opening: "She watched, from the chair beside her daughter."
  Key emotion: Trust, warmth, the generational weight of a Belgian ceremony.
  Format: Story Flow 10 companion — or a standalone Week 3 caption.
