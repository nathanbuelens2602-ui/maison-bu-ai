╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                   THE CAMPAIGN NARRATIVE                         ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

June 2026 arrives with something in the air.

Not urgency — something softer. The particular quality of light that
only happens in this month: long, warm, golden at the edges, alive
in a way that January light cannot imagine.

For the Maison BU guest — someone who carries a demanding and
stimulating world — June is the month when the weight lifts slightly.
The pace eases. The evenings stretch. She begins, quietly, to want
something beautiful for herself.

The Sweetness of June does not announce itself.
It arrives in the way the light falls through the atelier windows.
In the weight of a brushing before an evening out.
In the curl that settles — not into place, but into itself.

This campaign honours that sweetness.
It does not sell it. It reveals it.

The narrative arc across four weeks:

WEEK 1 — "The Light Returns"
Something has shifted in June. The campaign opens gently —
atmosphere before anything else. We invite the viewer into the
world of Maison BU in summer, establishing the emotional register.
No service mentions. Only feeling.

WEEK 2 — "The Ritual of Movement"
The craft arrives. Beachwaves, natural colour, the signature cut —
not as services, but as rituals of identity. The guest sees herself
in each piece of content and thinks: that is exactly what I need.

WEEK 3 — "The Ceremony"
June carries ceremony. Weddings, communions, end-of-year moments.
The atelier becomes the place where guests prepare for the moments
that matter. Not a rush-before-the-event atmosphere — a sacred
act of readying oneself.

WEEK 4 — "Before July"
The month closes. The emotional tone becomes quietly urgent —
not because of sales pressure, but because summer is finite and
beautiful, and a few moments in the atelier remain. The invitation
arrives naturally, after three weeks of desire have been created.

The guest who follows Maison BU through all of June should feel,
by the final post, that summer is no longer coming.
It has already begun — and she is part of it.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                   THE GUEST IN JUNE                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

She moves through a world still asking things of her.
But June shifts something. She allows herself to want — to feel good,
to be seen, to move through summer feeling exactly like herself.

She is preparing for a wedding where she will be seen by everyone
she knows. She is about to leave for ten days somewhere warm and
she wants her hair to behave beautifully without trying. She had
a difficult spring and this appointment at Maison BU is, quietly,
a gift she is giving herself.

What she wants in June:
— Beachwaves that look effortless on the beach and at a dinner table
— A colour that catches the summer light the way the sun does
— A brushing before the most important event of someone she loves
— The feeling, leaving the atelier, of being exactly herself —
  but the best version. The one summer calls forward.

What she is tired of:
— Salons that rush her to the next appointment
— Results that look beautiful in the chair but fall apart in the heat
— Being told what is trending rather than what is right for her
— Feeling like a client rather than a person

What Maison BU gives her in June that she cannot find elsewhere:
— The experience of being genuinely listened to
— Hair that holds its intention through heat, humidity, long evenings
— The feeling of leaving lighter than she arrived
— The knowledge that everything was crafted specifically for her


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║            JUNE 2026 EMOTIONAL TENSION MAP                       ║
║  (The invisible emotional landscape the content lives within)    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

The guest in June lives between:

FREEDOM ←————————————————————→ OBLIGATION
  (I want to slow down)         (Weddings, events, responsibilities)

EFFORTLESSNESS ←———————————————→ PREPARATION
  (I want hair that just is)     (I must look right for this moment)

DESIRE ←—————————————————————→ RESTRAINT
  (I want to indulge myself)     (Is this the right time? The right price?)

SUMMER ←——————————————————————→ THE CITY
  (I want to be somewhere warm)  (I am still here, moving through my life)

Maison BU lives in the resolution of these tensions.
The content must speak to both sides — never pretending the obligation
doesn't exist, never amplifying it into pressure.

The creative solution: beauty as relief.
Not escape — relief. The appointment at Maison BU does not take
the guest away from her life. It returns her to it, better equipped.

─── PRIMARY VISUAL WORLD ───────────────────────────────────────────

Reference universe:
  Yves Saint Laurent Summer — bold femininity softened by warm light
  Paris in June — the Marais at 8pm, the Seine at golden hour,
    limestone buildings catching late sun
  Jacquemus summer — skin, warmth, natural movement, sensory intimacy
  Loewe campaigns — extreme close-up, texture, intelligence
  Aesop visual language — restraint, concrete, considered beauty

Core visual qualities:
  Warmth above all. Every frame should feel warm.
  Movement — hair, fabric, curtains, light — everything breathes.
  Air — space between elements, never overcrowded.
  Skin — warm tones, summer skin, natural and unlaboured.
  Shadow — soft shadows, not harsh. Shadows that add depth, not drama.

─── COLOUR PALETTE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Primary:
  Warm gold (#C9923A tonal family) — the colour of June light
  Warm white (#F8F3ED) — clean, breathing, never clinical
  Architectural stone (#8E8578) — the atelier itself
  Rich dark (#1A1A18) — typography, borders, anchor tones

Accent for Week 3 (ceremony):
  Warm champagne (#E8D5B0) — the elegance of an event evening
  Deep rose-nude (#C4856A) — skin warmth in ceremonial content

Avoid:
  Cool blues or clinical whites — this is a warm month
  Electric or saturated tones — always muted, always film-like
  Black-and-white for video — June is in colour, full warmth
  (Grid static images may use warm monochrome — not cold)

─── LIGHTING DIRECTION ─────────────────────────────────────────────

Week 1 (The Light Returns):
  Soft window light, late morning. The quality of June at 10am.
  Natural diffused. No fill lights needed. Let the shadows exist softly.

Week 2 (The Ritual of Movement):
  Golden hour light. The atelier in late afternoon.
  The wand catches light. Hair catches light. Everything warm.

Week 3 (The Ceremony):
  More directional light — the quality of getting ready.
  A warm lamp. A mirror. The feeling of evening beginning.
  More intentional, more composed. A painting, not a snapshot.

Week 4 (Before July):
  The long evening light of June — 8pm feels like 5pm.
  The atelier in that last gold of the day. Rooms half in shadow,
  half in warm light. The feeling of a beautiful ending.

─── TEXTURES TO FEATURE ────────────────────────────────────────────

Hair: movement, weight, the way it catches light, texture not shine
Skin: natural, warm, not retouched into porcelain
Fabric: linen, silk, light cotton — the way summer clothes fall
Architecture: stone, concrete, glass — the bones of the atelier
Metal: scissors, the wand, dark metal accents — tools as objects
Water: only as suggestion — condensation, a wet brush, morning light
Shadow: particularly the soft edge of hair shadow on skin

─── WHAT IS NEVER SHOWN ───────────────────────────────────────────

  Ring lights (at all)
  Plastic chairs or visible salon equipment
  Chaotic styling stations or product overload
  Artificial before/after split-screen
  Posed "transformation" faces — no gasps, no mirror selfies
  Cheap summer aesthetics: palm trees, cocktail imagery, bikini references
  TikTok-style transitions or fast editing
  Text-heavy overlays or busy graphics
  Anything that looks like it was made quickly

║           APPROVED MUSIC DIRECTION — JUNE 2026                   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Philosophy:
  Music in Maison BU content shapes emotion — it never explains it.
  It should feel like the natural sound of the world being shown.
  Never high-energy. Never trend-led. Never recognisable in a way
  that distracts from the brand world.

─── WEEK 1 — Morning calm, philosophical ───────────────────────────

  Nils Frahm — "Says" / "All Melody"
  Ólafur Arnalds — "Near Light" / "Brot"
  Max Richter — "On the Nature of Daylight"
  Ludovico Einaudi — quieter works (Fly, Experience)
  Tempo: 50–65 BPM. No percussion. Pure piano or ambient.

─── WEEK 2 — Craft, movement, sensory ─────────────────────────────

  Sébastien Tellier — "La Ritournelle" (instrumental) / "Fantino"
  Air — "Ce Matin Là" / "La Femme d'Argent" (passages)
  Nicolas Jaar — quieter passages
  Jon Hopkins — "Immunity" (quieter sections, not the build)
  Tempo: 65–80 BPM. Some warmth. Subtle rhythm begins to emerge.

─── WEEK 3 — Ceremony, elegance, weight ──────────────────────────

  Erik Satie — Gymnopédies (orchestral versions)
  Chopin — Nocturnes (modern piano recordings)
  Yann Tiersen — quieter works
  French film scores: Claude Lelouch, Michel Legrand
  Tempo: 55–70 BPM. More emotional. More space between notes.

─── WEEK 4 — Freedom, golden hour, arrival ────────────────────────

  Sébastien Tellier — "Fantino" / "Look"
  Melody's Echo Chamber — ambient passages
  Charlotte Gainsbourg — quieter moments
  Air — "Cherry Blossom Girl" (instrumental)
  Bonobo — slow tracks (Kong, Kiara)
  Tempo: 70–85 BPM. Warmth increases. The feeling of summer.

─── UNIVERSAL MUSIC RULES ─────────────────────────────────────────

  Always:
    Volume sits beneath the visuals — underscore, not score
    Fade in and out clean — no hard cuts in music
    No lyrics (unless French and deeply ambient — sparingly)
    Match the tempo of the edit, not the other way around

  Never:
    Commercial trap, pop, or recognisable chart music
    Trend sounds from TikTok or Instagram audio
    High-energy EDM or anything that would belong to a sports brand
    Anything that makes the viewer think of a different world


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║              BOOKING PSYCHOLOGY STRUCTURE — JUNE 2026            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

The Maison BU booking psychology follows a four-phase structure
aligned with the four weeks of the campaign.

PHASE 1 — DESIRE CREATION (Week 1)
  Goal: Make the viewer feel the world of Maison BU in June.
  No booking ask. No service mention.
  The viewer builds an unconscious association: Maison BU = summer ease.
  This phase plants the emotional seed that grows into a booking by Week 4.

PHASE 2 — IDENTIFICATION (Week 2)
  Goal: The viewer sees themselves in the content.
  "That is what I want for my hair. That is how I want to feel."
  Service mentions arrive naturally, framed as desires not products.
  One soft CTA appears at the end of key captions — never in Stories.
  The viewer begins to consider booking without having been asked.

PHASE 3 — SOCIAL PROOF + CEREMONY (Week 3)
  Goal: Validate the desire through emotional moments.
  Wedding and communion content shows the stakes of the appointment.
  The guest sees: "People trust Maison BU for the most important days."
  This deepens trust without testimonials or reviews.
  Booking language: "Book your ceremony appointment. A few remain."

PHASE 4 — THE INVITATION (Week 4)
  Goal: Convert desire to booking through natural, warm invitation.
  The content tells the emotional truth: June is nearly over.
  Summer is here. A few appointments remain.
  The CTA is present in every piece — but always as the final line,
  always after the desire has been fully activated.
  Language: warm, composed, never urgent. Always an invitation.

The conversion principle:
  At no point in this structure does the viewer feel they are being
  sold to. They feel they are discovering something, being invited
  into something, and choosing it for themselves.
  The booking feels like their idea — because it is.

║                JUNE 2026 QUALITY-CONTROL CHECKLIST               ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Before publishing any June 2026 content, check every box:

PRIME DIRECTIVE
  ☐ Would the guest feel more human after seeing this — not impressed,
    not manipulated, but calmer, seen, elevated, emotionally lighter?

BRAND VOICE
  ☐ Read aloud: does it sound like a real person who genuinely cares?
  ☐ Could this belong to any other salon in Belgium? (If yes → rewrite)
  ☐ No forbidden words: luxury, glam, flawless, slay, obsessed, glow up,
    girls, viral, hurry, quick fix, deal, promo, discount, treat yourself

TONE
  ☐ Restrained — no exclamation marks, no over-excited language
  ☐ Warm — intimate, personal, like a confidante not a brand
  ☐ Cinematic — sensory, evocative, the reader can feel the atmosphere
  ☐ Timeless — nothing that will age in six months

VISUAL
  ☐ No ring lights visible in any frame
  ☐ No chaotic transitions or fast TikTok-style edits
  ☐ Colour grade is warm — never cold or clinical
  ☐ Music is underscore — never trendy or distracting
  ☐ Air and space exist in the frame — nothing overcrowded
  ☐ Summer feels urban and elegant — never beach-club or cheap

CAPTIONS
  ☐ Does not open with "We" or "Maison BU"
  ☐ Begins with a sensory or emotional hook — never with information
  ☐ Maximum 150 words for the main body
  ☐ No emoji (or one subtle exception at the very end, used rarely)
  ☐ Hashtags are curated — never generic beauty spam

STORIES
  ☐ Maximum 3 slides per flow
  ☐ Desire arrives before the booking link
  ☐ Text is minimal — one idea per slide
  ☐ Font is serif, white or warm off-white — never playful or casual
  ☐ No countdown stickers, no poll abuse, no sales urgency design

REELS
  ☐ No text on screen in first 5 seconds — let the image arrive
  ☐ Text overlay is minimal — one line, white serif, lower third
  ☐ Shot duration is long enough to feel atmosphere
  ☐ Music is present and considered — not an afterthought
  ☐ No moment in the reel that looks like a different brand

EMAIL
  ☐ Subject line sounds like a person, not a campaign
  ☐ Opening does not begin with information — begins with atmosphere
  ☐ One CTA only, at the end, after desire has been created
  ☐ Button copy: "Reserve your summer ritual" or equivalent
  ☐ Read aloud as Kristof: if it sounds human, it is ready

BOOKING CTAs
  ☐ Never appears before the desire has been created
  ☐ Never uses: hurry, now, last chance, don't miss, limited time
  ☐ Always uses: "receive," "reserve," "a moment remains," "a window"
  ☐ One CTA per piece — never two

THE FINAL QUESTION
  ☐ Does this feel like it could belong to Maison BU and only Maison BU?
  ☐ Would Kristof be proud of it?
  ☐ Would the guest feel seen by it — not sold to?

If every box is checked: publish.
If one box is unchecked: do not publish until it is resolved.
