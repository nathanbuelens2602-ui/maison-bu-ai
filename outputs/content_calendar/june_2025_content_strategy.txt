═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  MONTHLY NARRATIVE ARC
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

June is the month of softness. After the sharpness of spring,
something opens — the light stays longer, the air holds warmth,
and hair begins to move differently. This month, Maison BU
celebrates the quiet luxury of effortless beauty: movement over
structure, luminosity over drama, the feeling of arriving at
summer completely yourself.

The arc across four weeks: Arrival → Ritual → Identity → Invitation.
A guest who follows Maison BU through all of June should feel,
by the last post, that summer has already begun inside the atelier.

  CAMPAIGN IDENTITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Campaign name:   La Douceur de Juin
                 (The Gentleness of June)
Campaign feeling: Effortless. Luminous. Quietly alive.
Visual palette:  Warm gold, soft natural light, movement,
                 skin tones, soft blondes and deep naturals,
                 architectural details of the atelier in summer light
Music direction: Sébastien Tellier, Air, Nils Frahm — unhurried,
                 warm, European summer sensibility
Key services:    Summer hair · Beachwaves · Signature Cuts ·
                 Tailor-Made Brushings · Subtle Movement work
Belgian context: Wedding season · End of school year · Summer
                 social events · The first long evenings in Hasselt


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  KEY DATES & CULTURAL MOMENTS — JUNE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Week 1: First week of June — emotional "arrival of summer." Light
        shifts noticeably. Content should mirror this transition.

Mid-June: Belgian summer social season begins in earnest.
          Weddings, garden parties, summer events. Guests begin
          preparing. Brushing and beachwaves content most relevant.

Late June: Summer school events, graduations, end-of-year moments.
           Signature Cut content peaks here — renewal, identity,
           the feeling of stepping into a new chapter.

Throughout: Anchor all content in golden hour light and the feeling
            of effortless movement — never over-styled, always personal.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WEEK 1 — "The Light Has Changed"
  Emotional theme: Arrival. The first warmth. Something lifts.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Monday | The Ritual | Reel (30s)
  Visual: Extreme close-up of beachwaves being crafted — the wand,
          the release of a curl, warm studio light, hands in motion.
          Slow. Reverent. No sound but the soft ambient of the atelier.
  Copy direction: The process as ceremony. Opening line:
          "Some things should never be rushed."
  CTA: None — this is pure world-building.

Wednesday | The Edit | Carousel (5 slides)
  Visual: Clean editorial — each slide one image, one insight.
          White space. Architectural neutrals. Minimal text.
  Copy direction: "The Summer Hair Guide — from Maison BU."
          Slide 1: What summer does to hair (the science, poetically told).
          Slide 2: Beachwaves — the Maison BU approach.
          Slide 3: Brushing in summer vs winter.
          Slide 4: The Signature Cut as seasonal reset.
          Slide 5: "Book your summer ritual." Quiet CTA.
  CTA: Link in bio.

Friday | The Maison | Feed (static image)
  Visual: Kristof or a team member in the atelier — warm afternoon light,
          caught mid-consultation, natural, unposed.
  Copy direction: Kristof's philosophy on summer beauty.
          Opening line: "Every season asks something different of us."
  CTA: None.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WEEK 2 — "The Ritual of Movement"
  Emotional theme: Craft. Beachwaves as artistry, not technique.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Monday | The Transformation | Feed (before/after)
  Visual: Natural reveal — not dramatic, not split-screen.
          Two images: first, hair before; second, beachwaves finished.
          Both shot in the same warm light. The difference is felt.
  Copy direction: About the emotional shift, not the technique.
          Never use "before/after." Frame as: before she arrived,
          and after she stepped back into the light.
  CTA: "Reserve your moment."

Wednesday | The Ritual | Reel (15s)
  Visual: Hands-only. A tailor-made brushing in real time.
          The weight of the brush. The warmth of the dryer.
          The hair moving like fabric. Extreme close-up throughout.
  Copy direction: Two lines on screen only. No caption needed.
          Screen text: "Tailor-made." / "For you, specifically."
  CTA: None.

Thursday | L'Invitation | Story sequence (see Story Flow #2)
  "What to book before summer truly begins"
  3-slide story. Gentle, informative, atmospheric.

Friday | The Edit | Feed (single image)
  Visual: Detail shot — a finished beachwave in late afternoon light.
          Just the hair. Just the texture. Just the warmth.
  Copy direction: The Maison BU approach to movement.
          "Beachwaves at Maison BU are never about replicating the beach.
           They are about finding the version of you that belongs there."
  CTA: Link in bio to book.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WEEK 3 — "The Signature of June"
  Emotional theme: Identity. The Signature Cut as summer renewal.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Monday | The Transformation | Reel (45s)
  Visual: A full Signature Cut experience — consultation, the cut itself,
          the moment the chair turns to face the mirror.
          Cinematic. Unhurried. The guest's expression at the reveal.
  Copy direction: About the feeling of being truly seen and then
          released back into the world, lighter.
  CTA: "Book your Signature Cut." + booking link in caption.

Wednesday | The Maison | Feed
  Visual: Two images alternating — the atelier in summer light,
          and a close-up of scissors, comb, the tools of the craft.
  Copy direction: Kristof on the Signature Cut as couture.
          "Each cut is made for exactly one person.
           The one sitting in front of us."
  CTA: None — this is trust-building.

Friday | The Ritual | Story sequence (see Story Flow #3)
  A day inside Maison BU in June. Atmospheric, quiet, beautiful.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WEEK 4 — "La Douceur de Juin — L'Invitation"
  Emotional theme: Belonging. Summer is here. The atelier is ready.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Monday | L'Invitation | Feed
  Visual: The atelier in golden evening light — the space itself,
          waiting, still, beautiful. No people. Just the world.
  Copy direction: The month draws to a close. A few summer appointments
          remain. The tone is quiet, assured, never urgent.
  CTA: "A few summer moments remain in June.
        Reserve yours." + booking link.

Wednesday | The Transformation | Reel (30s)
  Visual: A montage of June — all the quiet moments from the month.
          Beachwaves. A brushing. A Signature Cut reveal. Light.
          A guest's smile. The atelier. The hands. The movement.
          Set to warm, slow music. The feeling of a month well-spent.
  Copy direction: Closing the campaign. Gratitude and invitation.
  CTA: "July awaits. Begin here." + booking link.

Friday | The Maison | Feed
  Visual: The team — warm, natural, caught in a genuine moment together.
  Copy direction: An end-of-month reflection from Kristof.
          Personal. Grateful. Not promotional. A letter, not a post.
  CTA: None.
