────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MAISON BU — REEL SCRIPT 02
  "The Lightest Version"
  Type: Transformation · Duration: 30 seconds
  Campaign: La Douceur de Juin
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL CONCEPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A reel about a Signature Cut — but the scissors are almost incidental.

What this reel is really about: the feeling of arriving at yourself
again. Of sitting in a chair and being listened to by someone
who understands what you haven't quite found the words for.
And then leaving, with something lighter. Not just your hair.

The entire 30 seconds moves from arrival to departure.
Two worlds: outside the atelier (busy, fast, stimulated) and
inside (still, warm, considered). The cut happens in between —
implied, not shown in full. The viewer fills it in with their own longing.

This reel should make someone in Hasselt — or anywhere — feel:
"That is exactly what I need. I didn't know it until now."


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  SHOT LIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[SHOT 1] 0:00–0:03 — HOOK
Outside the atelier. The historic façade in golden morning light.
A guest approaches the door — we see her from behind, unhurried,
a moment of intention before she enters. She pauses.
Camera: handheld tracking shot from slightly behind. Slow.
The focus shifts from the street to the door as she reaches for it.

[SHOT 2] 0:03–0:07
Inside. The door closes behind her. The noise of the street
disappears. The atelier receives her — warm, still, quiet.
She looks up. The space settles around her.
Camera: static medium shot from inside the atelier, facing the door.
The light changes as the door opens and closes.

[SHOT 3] 0:07–0:13
The consultation. Kristof (or the stylist) and the guest
at the mirror. Not speaking — or speaking quietly, privately.
The stylist's hands lift the guest's hair, observing, feeling the weight.
The guest watches in the mirror, alert, trusting.
Camera: static medium-wide, from the side. Patient. Uninterrupted.
Duration holds for 6 full seconds — long enough to feel the quality of attention.

[SHOT 4] 0:13–0:20
The cut begins. A close-up of scissors — clean, precise, unhurried.
The sound of the cut is satisfying and quiet. The hair falls slowly.
Then: a detail of hands combing through the length. The movement.
The alignment. The absolute intention of each gesture.
Camera: cuts between two macro shots. No fast editing — 3 seconds each.

[SHOT 5] 0:20–0:26
The reveal. The chair turns — but we do not see the mirror.
We see the guest's face first. A beat. A breath.
Then, subtly, the suggestion of a smile — but private.
Not performed. The recognition of someone who has been seen.
Camera: medium close-up from slightly behind and to the side.
The mirror is partially visible — her reflection is there,
but the focus stays on her face.

[SHOT 6] 0:26–0:30 — CLOSE
The guest leaving. The same door she entered.
But now from inside — we watch her step back into the light.
Her hair moves as the door opens. The light catches it.
She does not look back. She does not need to.
Camera: static. The camera stays inside the atelier as she departs.
The door closes. The atelier is still again. Hold for 2 seconds.

00:00–00:06  [No text. The world of the reel must land first.]

             "The Signature Cut."
             White serif. Tracked wide. Fades out after 2 seconds.

00:26–00:30  Final text appears as she reaches the door:
             "Crafted around the person, never the trend."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  AUDIO DIRECTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Music:
  Mood: contemplative, intimate, resolving — like a held breath
        that slowly releases into warmth
  Tempo: slow. 55–65 BPM.
  Reference tracks:
    — Nils Frahm, "All Melody" (opening passages)
    — Jon Hopkins, "Immunity" (quieter passages, not the build)
    — Ólafur Arnalds, "Near Light" or "So Far"
    — Angelo Badalamenti (Twin Peaks OST — the quieter pieces)
  Direction: The music should arrive with the guest and leave with her.
             When she steps back outside, it should feel like resolution.

Sound design:
  — The door opening at 0:00 — the sound of the street, briefly
  — The door closing at 0:03 — and then: complete interior quiet
  — The sound of scissors at 0:13 — real, clean, satisfying
  — The door opening again at 0:26 — the light sound of summer outside
  These sounds sit beneath the music, barely present.

No voiceover.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CAPTION COPY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

She arrived with something she couldn't quite name —
a feeling that her hair had been belonging to someone
else for a while.

The consultation at Maison BU is not about what we want
to create. It is about what already exists within the person
sitting in front of us — and how to reveal it more clearly.

The Signature Cut does not begin with scissors.
It begins with listening.

When she left, she paused at the door and looked back,
just once. Not at the mirror. At the light.

Crafted around the person, never the trend.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  PRODUCTION NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Lens:
  35mm for the wide/medium shots — the atelier architecture, the door,
  the full consultation. Keeps perspective natural and immersive.
  85mm for the close details — scissors, hands, the guest's face.

Colour grade:
  Two worlds — one visual language.
  Exterior shots (door, approach): slightly cooler, the city world.
  Interior shots: immediately warmer, golden, enveloping.
  The contrast should feel like stepping into a different emotional register.
  Not dramatic — subtle. The warmth of the atelier wraps around the viewer.

Sound:
  The silence inside the atelier is the most important sound in the reel.
  It must feel immediately different from the exterior.
  If sound design is used, this contrast is critical.

Must-capture moments:
  — The guest's hand on the door, about to enter (the decision)
  — The stylist lifting the guest's hair during consultation (the attention)
  — The moment of recognition in the guest's face during the reveal
  — The hair catching the light as she leaves (the result, felt not announced)

What to avoid:
  — A triumphant reveal (no spinning chair, no gasp, no "wow")
  — Scissors moving too fast or carelessly
  — Any moment that looks like it was directed by the subject
  — A commercial ending — no logo slam, no call to action on screen
  — Music that builds to a peak — this reel resolves, it does not climax
