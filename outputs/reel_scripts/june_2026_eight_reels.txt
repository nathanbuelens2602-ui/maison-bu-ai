────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MAISON BU · THE SWEETNESS OF JUNE · 2026
  8 Cinematic Reel Concepts
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Production philosophy across all 8 reels:
  Lens: 85mm or 100mm for close-ups. 35mm for atmosphere.
  Grade: Warm, filmic. Reference — Loewe SS, Aesop, Jacquemus.
  Edit pace: No cut faster than 3 seconds. Let the viewer breathe.
  Text: Arrives late, leaves early. White serif. Tracked wide.
  Sound: Music first. Sound design beneath. Never voiceover.
  Never: ring lights, fast transitions, chaotic energy, TikTok framing.


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 01 — "The Atelier at June"
  Week: 1  ·  Type: The Maison  ·  Duration: 45 seconds
  Goal: Open the campaign world. No service. Pure atmosphere.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
The first reel of June does not show a haircut.
It shows what June does to the Maison BU atelier.
The light through the windows. The stone absorbing warmth.
The team moving through the space in that particular morning calm
before the first guest arrives. A reel that makes the viewer feel:
"I want to be inside that world."

SHOT LIST
[01] 0:00–0:05 — HOOK
  Close-up: morning light falling through a tall window onto the
  concrete floor of the atelier. The light moves imperceptibly.
  Dust particles drift through the shaft of light. Still and alive.
  Camera: static macro. Rack focus from the floor to the light itself.

[02] 0:05–0:10
  The window frame — architectural, historic — seen from inside.
  Beyond: Hasselt in June morning light. The city is quiet.
  Camera: static wide. The frame breathes.

[03] 0:10–0:17
  Kristof (or a team member) moves through the atelier — not for
  the camera. They are arranging tools, setting up for the day.
  Their hands. Their intention. Their unhurried pace.
  Camera: slow tracking shot, medium distance. They do not look up.

[04] 0:17–0:24
  Close-up of the stylist's station — tools laid out with care.
  Scissors. A comb. A brush. The tools of the craft, arranged
  with the same attention as a painter's palette.
  Camera: static macro. Extremely slow pull-back to reveal context.

[05] 0:24–0:33
  The atelier chair — empty, waiting, in warm morning light.
  Not clinical. Not commercial. Sacred in its stillness.
  Camera: medium shot from the side. The chair faces the window.
  June light falls across the leather. Hold for 6 full seconds.

[06] 0:33–0:40
  The space in full — the widest shot in the reel.
  The atelier: concrete, stone, wood, dark metal, glass.
  Morning light fills the space. No one is in this shot.
  The space itself is the subject.
  Camera: static wide from the door. The atelier breathes.

[07] 0:40–0:45 — CLOSE
  Return to the original shot: light on the concrete floor.
  It has moved slightly since Shot 01. June time.
  Text fades in.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
  00:40 — "The Sweetness of June. / Maison BU · Hasselt"
  White serif. Tracked wide. Centred. Fades in over 1.5 seconds.
  Holds for 4 seconds. Fades clean.

AUDIO
  Music: Nils Frahm — "Says" (opening two minutes)
  Direction: Begin at zero volume, rise slowly to 40% by shot 03.
  The silence at the beginning is intentional — the image arrives first.
  Sound design: faint ambient of the building — nothing more.

CAPTION
  Before the first guest arrives.
  Before the first appointment begins.
  This is what the atelier looks like in June.

  Warm. Still. Ready.
  The way it is every morning — and the reason guests feel it
  the moment they step inside.

  The Sweetness of June begins here.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 02 — "The Beachwaves Ritual"
  Week: 2  ·  Type: The Ritual  ·  Duration: 45 seconds
  Goal: Beachwaves as ceremony. Desire before information.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
The entire reel takes place in close-up.
No faces. No wide shots. No smiling to camera.
Only: hands, hair, tools, warmth, texture.
The beachwave as a meditative act.
The viewer should feel the warmth of the wand, smell the heat,
watch the curl find itself — and think: I want that.

SHOT LIST
[01] 0:00–0:04 — HOOK
  Extreme macro: a single strand of hair being wrapped around
  a wand. The metal is warm. The hair is warm-toned and natural.
  The strand catches the light as it wraps. Not salon-lit —
  window-lit. Warm and alive.
  Camera: macro static. Rack focus from hand to hair strand.

[02] 0:04–0:10
  The curl releasing from the wand. Slow motion (120fps at 50%).
  It falls. It settles. It catches the light. It finds itself.
  Nothing is placed. Everything is revealed.
  Camera: static macro. The most important shot in the reel.

[03] 0:10–0:17
  Pull back slightly — we see both hands working now.
  One holds the section, one guides the wand. Ballet-like.
  The guest's profile is barely visible — soft focus, background.
  Window light from the left. Gold.
  Camera: slow pull-back, handheld but controlled.

[04] 0:17–0:24
  Close-up of a section of finished beachwaves in raking light.
  Not a mirror shot. The hair itself.
  Each wave individual but belonging to a whole — like a score.
  Camera: static macro. The light catches each wave differently.

[05] 0:24–0:31
  The stylist's fingers move through the finished waves —
  separating them with the lightest touch. Silk through silk.
  Camera: slow tracking left to right, following the hands.

[06] 0:31–0:38
  The guest tilts her head slightly. We see her in profile —
  soft focus. Her eyes are closed. Her breathing is unhurried.
  She is not performing. She is simply present.
  Camera: static medium shot from behind the station, angled.

[07] 0:38–0:45 — CLOSE
  Return to the first extreme close-up: a single finished wave
  catching the afternoon light. The whole reel contained in one curl.
  Text arrives.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
  00:39 — "Not every wave is made the same."
  White serif. Lower third. Slim and tracked. Fade in.
  00:43 — "Maison BU · Hasselt"
  Smaller. Centred. Same typeface. Fades last.

AUDIO
  Music: Sébastien Tellier — "La Ritournelle" (instrumental section)
  Direction: warm, unhurried, European summer evening feeling.
  Sound design: the faint sound of the wand barrel, the warmth —
  barely audible beneath the music. Enough to feel it.

CAPTION
  The wand lifts. The curl releases.
  Not into place — but into itself.

  Beachwaves at Maison BU are never a formula.
  They are a conversation: about the weight of your hair,
  the shape of your face, the way you move through the world.
  About what effortless means to you specifically.

  The result should look like nothing happened.
  Only that something settled.

  Link in bio — summer rituals available in June.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 03 — "A Summer Signature Cut"
  Week: 2  ·  Type: The Transformation  ·  Duration: 30 seconds
  Goal: The Signature Cut as identity work. Desire through story.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
A Signature Cut told through the consultation alone.
No scissors until the final second. No reveal.
The entire emotional weight of the reel lives in the quality
of attention given before anything begins.
This reel makes the viewer think: "I have never been listened to
like that. I want to know what that feels like."

SHOT LIST
[01] 0:00–0:04 — HOOK
  A hand — Kristof's or a senior artisan's — lifting the guest's
  hair at the base of the neck. Weighing it. Feeling it.
  Not clinical. Sacred.
  Camera: extreme close-up, static. The hand is the subject.

[02] 0:04–0:10
  The consultation. Both at the mirror.
  The artisan speaks quietly. The guest watches.
  Their eyes meet in the mirror — not performing, simply present.
  Camera: medium-wide from the side. Static. 6 seconds. Hold.

[03] 0:10–0:17
  Close-up of the artisan's hands parting the hair, observing
  sections, the architecture of the head being understood.
  Camera: slow tracking, following the hands. No rush.

[04] 0:17–0:22
  The guest in profile, eyes slightly lowered. Not performing.
  The light from the window is warm on her cheek and her hair.
  She looks like someone who has been genuinely seen.
  Camera: soft static medium. She doesn't know she's being filmed.

[05] 0:22–0:26
  The first snip. One clean, decisive cut.
  The sound of scissors — real, clean, quiet and decisive.
  Camera: macro on the scissors and the falling strand of hair.
  Slow motion. The strand catches the light as it falls.

[06] 0:26–0:30 — CLOSE
  The guest opens her eyes. Looks at herself.
  Not a reaction. A recognition. A quiet breath.
  The text arrives as she looks.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
  00:26 — "The Signature Cut."
  00:28 — "Crafted around the person, never the trend."
  White serif. One line at a time. Lower third. Fade clean.

AUDIO
  Music: Ólafur Arnalds — "Near Light"
  Direction: The piano enters at shot 02. The emotional weight of
  the music should match the weight of the consultation.
  No sound design needed — the music is enough.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 04 — "Colour as Light"
  Week: 2  ·  Type: The Ritual  ·  Duration: 30 seconds
  Goal: Colour work positioned as artistry, not technical service.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
Colour is not about changing hair.
It is about changing the quality of light that moves through it.
This reel shows colour as painting — the brush, the sections,
the foils, the rinse — all as moments of artistic intention.
The viewer does not see a "colour appointment."
They see a painter at work.

SHOT LIST
[01] 0:00–0:04 — HOOK
  Extreme close-up: a colour brush being loaded with tint.
  The colour itself — a warm, dimensional tone — against the bowl.
  Light catches it. It looks like pigment in oil.
  Camera: macro static.

[02] 0:04–0:10
  The brush meets hair. The stroke is fluid, decisive, artistic.
  We are in extreme close-up — no context, only the moment of
  colour meeting hair. The movement of the brush is beautiful.
  Camera: slow tracking, following the brush.

[03] 0:10–0:16
  A section of hair held up to the window light — pre-colour.
  Then: the same section after colour, in the same light.
  Not a before/after. Two moments of the same thing seen differently.
  Camera: static. The window is the light source. The difference is felt.

[04] 0:16–0:22
  The rinse. Warm water moving through coloured hair.
  Colour releasing. The drain. Then: clean hair, wet, in warm light.
  Camera: close-up, slow. The water catches light and moves.

[05] 0:22–0:26
  The finished colour — dry, styled, in the atelier's afternoon light.
  Extreme close-up of the hair surface: dimensional, alive, warm.
  Not a mirror selfie. The hair itself as the object of beauty.
  Camera: macro static. Hold for 4 full seconds.

[06] 0:26–0:30 — CLOSE
  The brush — clean now, resting beside the bowl.
  The work is done. The tool rests.
  Text arrives.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
  00:26 — "Colour that moves with the light."
  White serif. Lower third. Clean fade.
  00:28 — "Maison BU · Hasselt"

AUDIO
  Music: Nils Frahm — "All Melody" (opening section, piano only)
  Direction: unhurried, beautiful, emotional without being dramatic.


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 05 — "The Voice of Kristof"
  Week: 1 or 3  ·  Type: The Maison  ·  Duration: 45 seconds
  Goal: Founder philosophy. Trust-building. Brand depth.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
Kristof speaks — but not to camera. He is seen in the atelier,
working or moving through the space, while his words appear on
screen as text. His voice is not the selling voice.
It is the voice of someone who has spent years thinking deeply
about what he does and why it matters.
This reel makes people trust Maison BU before they have ever visited.

SHOT LIST
[01] 0:00–0:06 — HOOK
  Kristof's profile in the atelier — not posed. Caught mid-thought,
  perhaps looking at a guest's hair before consultation, or standing
  at the window in the morning. Natural, human, intelligent.
  Camera: static medium. He does not address the camera.

[02] 0:06–0:15
  His hands — working. Close-up of a consultation in motion.
  Lifting, feeling, observing hair with absolute attention.
  Camera: slow close-up tracking, following the hands.
  The first line of text appears during this shot.

[03] 0:15–0:24
  Kristof at the mirror with a guest (seen from the side, partial).
  He listens. The quality of his listening is visible.
  Camera: static medium from behind. Patient. Unhurried.
  Second line of text during this shot.

[04] 0:24–0:33
  Close-up of finished work — hair in the atelier light.
  His work. The result of the listening.
  Camera: static macro. The third and fourth lines of text appear.

[05] 0:33–0:45 — CLOSE
  Return to the profile of Kristof.
  He steps away from the mirror — the work is done.
  He doesn't look back. The guest's joy is implied, not shown.
  Final text and Maison BU title card.

ON-SCREEN TEXT (Kristof's words, appearing line by line)
  "We never begin with what we want to create."    (shot 02)
  "We begin with understanding the person."        (shot 02)
  "Because beauty that doesn't belong to the person — "  (shot 03)
  "doesn't belong anywhere."                       (shot 03)
  "The Signature Cut. Maison BU · Hasselt"         (shot 05)

AUDIO
  Music: Max Richter — "On the Nature of Daylight"
  No voiceover — Kristof's words live only on screen.
  The music carries the emotional weight of his philosophy.


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 06 — "Before the Ceremony"
  Week: 3  ·  Type: The Transformation  ·  Duration: 45 seconds
  Goal: Wedding/event preparation as intimate ritual.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
The morning before a wedding. Not the wedding itself.
Maison BU does not appear in the ceremony photographs.
It appears in the hour before — the ritual of getting ready,
the quiet intimacy of a brushing or an updo, the moment
when a woman looks at herself and recognises who she is today.
This reel is not about weddings. It is about devotion.
The devotion of a team to a person on their most important day.

SHOT LIST
[01] 0:00–0:04 — HOOK
  Close-up of hands — a guest's hands — holding a bouquet
  (or a glass of water, or simply resting on the armrests).
  She is seated in the Maison BU chair. The atelier is still.
  Camera: macro static. We feel her stillness.

[02] 0:04–0:12
  The artisan begins — pinning, brushing, crafting the hair.
  Slow, deliberate, reverential. Each pin placed with intention.
  Camera: medium shot from behind. The mirror shows her face — soft.
  She is not looking at her hair. She is somewhere else. Present.

[03] 0:12–0:20
  Close-up details: a section being twisted and pinned,
  a curl set with warmth, the architecture of the finished style
  beginning to emerge. Not rushed. Time exists differently here.
  Camera: alternating macro shots, each 4 seconds minimum.

[04] 0:20–0:30
  The stylist steps back. They observe. They adjust one strand.
  The quiet perfectionism of someone who cares.
  Camera: medium shot. The guest and the artisan in the frame together.
  No words. Just attention.

[05] 0:30–0:38
  The guest looks at herself in the mirror.
  Not the triumphant reveal — the private recognition.
  Her eyes move across her own face and hair.
  Something settles. She is ready.
  Camera: soft medium, slightly from the side. Her face in the mirror.

[06] 0:38–0:45 — CLOSE
  The atelier door. She opens it and steps into the light.
  We stay inside. The door closes.
  The atelier is still again. Ready for the next person.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
  00:35 — "The morning before."
  00:38 — "Maison BU · Hasselt"
  White serif. Quiet. The understated title of an important day.

AUDIO
  Music: Erik Satie — Gymnopédie No. 1 (modern orchestral recording)
  Direction: The music should feel like the morning itself —
  composed, unhurried, slightly emotional but never sentimental.


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 07 — "After the Storm"
  Week: 2 or 4  ·  Type: The Maison  ·  Duration: 30 seconds
  Goal: A June storm as metaphor for the atelier as refuge.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
June storms. They arrive without warning and leave the air
clean and electric. This reel captures a moment in the atelier
during a summer rain — the world outside grey and moving,
the world inside warm and still.
The storm does not disrupt Maison BU. It frames it.
The atelier as the one place where nothing is rushed.

SHOT LIST
[01] 0:00–0:04 — HOOK
  Close-up of a window: rain on glass, the street outside blurred
  and moving. Inside: the window frame, warm light, stillness.
  Camera: macro static. The contrast between outside and inside
  is felt immediately.

[02] 0:04–0:10
  Pull back slowly to reveal the atelier interior.
  A guest is in the chair. An artisan works — unhurried, calm.
  Outside, the rain. Inside, the ritual continues.
  Camera: slow pull-back from window to full atelier view.

[03] 0:10–0:18
  Close-up of hands working through hair — the same calm as always.
  Nothing about the storm outside changes the quality of attention.
  Camera: macro on the hands. The rain is audible in the background.

[04] 0:18–0:24
  The guest's profile. Her eyes are closed.
  Through the window behind her, lightning suggests itself —
  not dramatically, just a shift in the light.
  She doesn't open her eyes.
  Camera: medium, static. The moment of complete trust.

[05] 0:24–0:30 — CLOSE
  The rain begins to ease. Soft light returns to the window.
  The world outside settles. Inside, nothing changed.
  Text arrives.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
  00:26 — "Whatever the world outside —"
  00:28 — "the atelier remains."
  Maison BU · Hasselt"
  White serif. Emotional. Clean.

AUDIO
  Music: Ólafur Arnalds — "Brot" or "Near Light"
  Sound design: the rain, present and beautiful, beneath the music.
  The rain is part of the sound design — not edited out.


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL 08 — "The Sweetness of June" (Campaign Closing Reel)
  Week: 4  ·  Type: L'Invitation  ·  Duration: 60 seconds
  Goal: Close the campaign. Create belonging. Invite gently.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONCEPT
The campaign closing reel. A montage — but not a commercial montage.
A poem in images. All the quiet moments of June in the atelier:
the light, the hands, the hair, the guests, the tools, the space.
Set to the most beautiful piece of music in the campaign playlist.
The text arrives only at the very end.
By the time the booking link appears, the viewer is already inside.

SHOT LIST — each shot held 5–7 seconds minimum

[01] 0:00–0:06 — HOOK
  The atelier window at golden hour. Long.

[02] 0:06–0:12
  Hands lifting a section of hair in warm light.

[03] 0:12–0:18
  A finished beachwave catching the light.

[04] 0:18–0:24
  Kristof in the atelier — his back to us. Thinking.

[05] 0:24–0:30
  A guest's profile, eyes closed, in the chair. At peace.

[06] 0:30–0:36
  The tools — scissors, comb, brush — on the station.

[07] 0:36–0:42
  The atelier at evening light — the latest it can get in June.
  Everything gold. The space almost breathing.

[08] 0:42–0:50
  A guest leaving — stepping out the door into summer light.
  Her hair moves. She does not look back.

[09] 0:50–0:60 — CLOSE
  The door closing.
  The atelier in stillness.
  Text arrives, slowly, the final words of June.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
  00:50 — "The Sweetness of June."
  00:53 — "A few moments remain."
  00:56 — "The atelier is ready."
  00:58 — "Maison BU · Hasselt · maisonbu.be/book"
  Each line fades in separately. White serif. Unhurried.
  The booking link is the only functional information in the entire reel.
  It arrives last. After everything else has been felt.

AUDIO
  Music: Nils Frahm — "Says" (full arc, if licensing allows) or
         Ólafur Arnalds — "Near Light" (the extended version)
  Direction: The music should arc with the reel — begin quietly,
  swell gently through the middle montage, and resolve cleanly
  at the end. Never peaks. Always returns to calm.
  This is the most musically considered reel of the campaign.
  Spend time on the audio mix. It carries the whole.

CAPTION
  June.

  The light in the atelier stays warm until nearly nine.
  The hair moves differently in summer — lighter, more itself.
  The guests leave carrying something they didn't arrive with.

  That is what June means at Maison BU.

  The Sweetness of June is nearly over.
  A few appointments remain before July.
  We would be glad to receive you.
