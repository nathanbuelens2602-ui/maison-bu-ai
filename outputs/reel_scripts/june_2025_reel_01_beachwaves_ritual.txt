═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REEL CONCEPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A single morning in the atelier. A single guest.
A ritual that moves at the speed of intention.

This reel does not explain what beachwaves are.
It makes the viewer feel what it is to receive them —
the warmth of someone's hands in your hair, the weight
of the wand, the release of each curl, and the final
quiet moment when the guest and the mirror simply look
at each other.

The emotional promise: effortless beauty is not an accident.
It is the result of someone who cared enough to make it look that way.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  SHOT LIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[SHOT 1] 0:00–0:04 — HOOK
Extreme close-up of a single strand of hair being wrapped
around the wand. The metal catches the studio light. The hair
is warm-toned, natural, slightly damp from the brushing.
Camera: static, macro lens, rack focus from the wand to the hair.

[SHOT 2] 0:04–0:09
Pull back slowly to reveal the stylist's hands — controlled,
deliberate, graceful. The angle is from above and slightly behind.
We see the texture of the hair, the movement of wrists.
Nothing rushed. Everything decided.
Camera: slow pull-back, handheld but steady.

[SHOT 3] 0:09–0:16
Cut to the guest's profile — eyes closed, slightly tilted
toward the light from the window. Not performing. Simply resting.
The atelier is visible behind her: concrete, soft light, stillness.
Camera: static. The camera breathes but does not move.

[SHOT 4] 0:16–0:24
Return to hands. A second section released from the wand.
The curl falls in slow motion — we watch it settle, catch the light,
find its place among the others. Golden. Alive.
Camera: static macro. Slow motion (120fps played at 50%).

[SHOT 5] 0:24–0:32
Wide shot of the full atelier treatment station — the guest,
the stylist, the tools laid out with intention, the warm light
filling the room at an angle. Beautiful and quiet as a painting.
Camera: static wide. Let the scene breathe for 4 full seconds.

[SHOT 6] 0:32–0:40
The stylist's fingers move through the finished waves —
separating them gently, adding the final form. Extreme close-up
of hair moving between fingers. Silk-like. The texture is everything.
Camera: slow tracking from left to right, following the hands.

[SHOT 7] 0:40–0:45 — CLOSE
The guest opens her eyes. She looks at herself in the mirror.
No words. No reaction performed for the camera.
Just the quiet recognition of someone who has been seen.
Camera: medium shot, from behind the guest, the mirror shows her face.
The frame holds for 3 full seconds before the text fades in.

  AUDIO DIRECTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Music:
  Mood: warm, unhurried, European summer evening
  Tempo: slow (60–70 BPM)
  Reference tracks:
    — Sébastien Tellier, "La Ritournelle" (instrumental passages)
    — Air, "Ce matin là"
    — Nils Frahm, "Says"
    — Ólafur Arnalds, "Near Light"
  Direction: Begin quietly, let the music breathe with the images.
             Never let it peak. It should feel like the room's own warmth.

Sound design (layered underneath):
  — The soft pull of a brush through hair at 00:00
  — The faint ambient of the atelier — barely audible
  — The release of the wand's barrel at 00:04 (a soft click)
  — Complete silence would also work — the music alone is enough

No voiceover.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CAPTION COPY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The wand lifts. The curl releases.
It falls — not into place, but into itself.

Beachwaves at Maison BU are never about the beach.
They are about finding the version of you that moves
through summer with the quiet confidence of someone
who has never once tried too hard.

Each section, considered.
Each wave, placed with intention.
The result should look as though nothing happened —
only that something settled.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  PRODUCTION NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Lens: 85mm or 100mm macro for close-ups. 35mm for wider shots.
      No wide-angle distortion near the face or hands.

Colour grade:
  Warm, filmic. Slightly desaturated highlights. Golden shadows.
  Lift the orange-yellow channel in skin tones and hair.
  Reference grade: Loewe SS campaign, or Aesop product films.
  No cold tones. No clinical white balance.

Lighting:
  Primary: natural window light from camera left, diffused.
  Fill: a warm reflector only — no artificial key lights visible.
  No ring lights. No beauty dish. Never clinical or flat.

Must-capture moments:
  — The exact moment the curl is released from the wand (slow motion essential)
  — The guest's face in natural profile light — unposed
  — The texture of finished beachwaves in raking window light

What to avoid:
  — Any moment that looks "salon" — rushed, clinical, commercial
  — Forced smiles or posed reactions
  — The camera moving too fast at any point
  — Overhead ring-light reflections in mirrors or eyes
  — Any visual element that could belong to a different brand
