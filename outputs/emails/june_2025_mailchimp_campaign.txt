═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS
  (ranked: most personal → most evocative)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Option A (personal, intimate):
  "June is the month I think of you."

Option B (poetic, seasonal):
  "The light in the atelier has changed."

Option C (direct elegance):
  "Your summer ritual, before July."

Recommended: Option B
Reason: Creates immediate sensory curiosity. Implies change,
        invitation and intimacy without naming a service or an offer.
        The reader thinks: "What do they mean? I want to know."


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  PREVIEW TEXT OPTIONS
  (paired with Option B subject line)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Option A: "Warmer. Slower. The way we love it here."
Option B: "Summer hair begins with one quiet decision."
Option C: "A few moments remain in June. One of them is yours."

Recommended: Option A — it completes the subject line as a single
             sensory thought. The reader already feels inside the atelier
             before they open the email.

  EMAIL BODY
  Full version — plain text / rich text
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[Salutation]
To you, wherever June finds you —

[Opening]
The light in the Maison BU atelier does something particular this month.

It arrives earlier and stays later. It falls through the windows at an angle
we only see in June — warm and slightly gold, the kind that makes everything
look the way it should. The stone holds it. The mirrors return it, softened.

We have been moving through the days here with particular attention.
June asks us to slow down, and we have listened.

[Body — The Craft]
This month at Maison BU, we have been thinking about effortlessness.

Not simplicity — effortlessness. There is a difference.
Effortlessness requires mastery. It requires knowing exactly what to do,
and more importantly, what to leave undone.

That philosophy shapes everything we are offering this June:
beachwaves crafted specifically around your hair, your face, your movement —
never a formula, always a conversation. Signature Cuts that arrive at the
person rather than the trend. Tailor-made brushings for the evenings
that deserve to begin with intention.

Summer hair, in the Maison BU way, should feel as though it simply happened.
As though you woke up like this, or stepped in from somewhere beautiful.
The craft is ours. The ease is yours.

[The Invitation]
A few June appointments remain. We would love to receive you before the month closes
and the calendar fills for July.

[CTA Button]
Reserve your summer ritual

[Link: https://maisonbu.be/book]

[Sign-off]
With warmth from the atelier —

Kristof & the Maison BU team
Hasselt · Belgium

  MAILCHIMP SETUP NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Send time:       Tuesday or Wednesday, 9:00–10:00 AM
                 (when the guest is past the Monday rush but not yet
                 in the Friday wind-down)

Audience:        All active subscribers + previous clients
Segment option:  Test sending Option A subject to clients who haven't
                 booked in 2+ months (re-engagement angle)

A/B test:        Subject lines A vs B — measure open rate
                 Subject A ("June is the month I think of you") will
                 likely perform better with loyal clients.
                 Subject B ("The light in the atelier has changed")
                 will likely draw in cooler leads through curiosity.

Preview:         Send a test to Kristof first. Read it aloud.
                 If it sounds like a person, it is ready.
                 If it sounds like a brand, rewrite it.
