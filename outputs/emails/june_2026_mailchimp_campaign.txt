To you —

June does something particular to the atelier.

The light enters at a different angle than it did in May.
Warmer. More golden at the edges. The stone holds it differently —
longer, deeper. The mirrors return it softer than they did in spring.

We have been moving through this month with particular attention.
Not because of what it means commercially, but because of what
it means atmospherically. June has its own quality. Its own pace.
And that quality shapes everything we do inside these walls.

———

This is the month of movement.

Hair that lifts in the warmth. Beachwaves crafted specifically
around the person wearing them — their texture, their face, the way
they move through a summer evening. Colour that does what summer
light does: amplifies what is already there without announcing itself.
The Signature Cut, reshaped for the season — lighter, freer,
belonging completely to July and August and the weeks beyond.

And the ceremonies. The weddings, the communions, the evenings
that ask something of the people attending them.
June carries more ceremony than any other month in Belgium.
We have been part of those mornings quietly, reverently —
as we always are.

———

A few appointments remain before June closes and July begins.

We are not in the business of pressure. You know this.
But we are in the business of making sure the people who
wish they had come — come, while there is still time.

If June has been the month you meant to arrive —
it still is.

The atelier is ready.
The team is here.
And we would be glad to receive you.

Reserve your summer ritual →
maisonbu.be/book

With warmth from the atelier —

Kristof & the Maison BU team
Hasselt · Belgium
