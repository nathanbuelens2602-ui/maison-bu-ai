────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MAISON BU · THE SWEETNESS OF JUNE · 2026
  16 Instagram Story Flows — 4 per week
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Format per flow: 3 slides in sequence.
Visual: warm, editorial, minimal. One idea per slide.
Typography: white or warm off-white serif. Tracked. Restrained.
No countdown stickers. No poll abuse. No sales urgency design.
Link stickers only on the final slide, and only when the desire
has been fully created in slides 1 and 2.
