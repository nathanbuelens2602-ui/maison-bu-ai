Each Story Flow = 3 slides in sequence.
Format per slide: [VISUAL] / [TEXT ON SCREEN] / [INTERACTION if any]
Typography: White or warm off-white. Serif typeface. Minimal.
Design principle: Air. Stillness. One idea per slide.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  STORY FLOW 01 — "The Art of Beachwaves"
  Pillar: The Ritual
  Goal: Show craft. Create desire. No CTA until the very end.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

— SLIDE 1 —
Visual: Extreme close-up of a single beachwave falling into place.
        The hair catches the light. Slow motion if possible.
        Warm, golden — almost amber. No faces. Just hair and hands.

Text on screen:
  "Not every wave is made the same."

Placement: Lower third. White serif. Fade in slowly.

─

— SLIDE 2 —
Visual: A wider shot — the stylist's hands sectioning the hair,
        the wand positioned with precision, the guest's head tilted
        naturally toward the window light. Calm, focused, unhurried.

Text on screen:
  "At Maison BU, beachwaves begin with a conversation.
   About who you are, and how you want to move through summer."

Placement: Centred. Two lines. Subtle white.

─

— SLIDE 3 —
Visual: The finished result — not a mirror shot, not a posed portrait.
        The guest touching her own hair, softly, looking somewhere
        off-camera. Natural. Private. The feeling of recognition.

Text on screen:
  "Summer hair, crafted around you."
  [Below, after a breath]: "Reserve your ritual →"

Link sticker: maisonbu.be/book
Placement: Text centred. Link sticker bottom centre.

  STORY FLOW 02 — "What to Book Before Summer"
  Pillar: The Edit + L'Invitation
  Goal: Educate gently. Guide the guest toward the right service.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

— SLIDE 1 —
Visual: The atelier in morning light — empty, still, architectural.
        The treatment stations clean and ready. A quiet, considered space.

Text on screen:
  "Before summer arrives —
   a guide from the Maison BU atelier."

Placement: Upper third. Warm white serif.

─

— SLIDE 2 —
Visual: Three soft images in sequence — or a single editorial collage.
        Each paired with a service name and its emotional purpose.
        Clean white background between each. Restrained.

Text on screen (staggered, one per breath):

  "The Signature Cut.
   For the version of you that summer calls forward."

  [pause]

  "Beachwaves.
   Crafted around your hair, your face, your movement."

  [pause]

  "The Tailor-Made Brushing.
   For evenings that deserve to begin well."

Typography: Centred. Refined. Each line appears separately.

─

— SLIDE 3 —
Visual: Close-up of the booking interface — or simply the atelier chair
        in golden light with the booking link overlaid cleanly.

Text on screen:
  "June still holds a few moments.
   The atelier is ready to receive you."

Link sticker: maisonbu.be/book
Poll sticker (optional): "Which ritual calls to you this summer?"
  Option A: Signature Cut
  Option B: Beachwaves


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  STORY FLOW 03 — "A Morning in the Atelier"
  Pillar: The Maison
  Goal: Intimacy. Let the world in — without losing elegance.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

— SLIDE 1 —
Visual: The atelier before opening. Kristof arranging tools,
        or simply the space in that particular morning quiet.
        Natural light. No styling. No performance.

Text on screen:
  "8:47. Before the first guest arrives."

Placement: Bottom left. Small. White. Almost like a timestamp.

─

— SLIDE 2 —
Visual: A short video (5–8 seconds). The espresso being made.
        The mirrors catching early light. Someone reading something.
        The sound of the city faintly outside, muffled by the atelier.

Text on screen:
  "This is what happens before the work begins.
   The settling. The quiet. The readiness."

Placement: Centred. Two lines. White serif.

─

— SLIDE 3 —
Visual: A close-up of Kristof's hands — setting out tools, or
        simply resting on the back of the chair. Intentional stillness.

Text on screen:
  "Every guest deserves someone who was ready for them."

Placement: Lower third. Calm. No link. No CTA.
           This slide asks for nothing. It only offers.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  STORY FLOW 04 — "A Few Moments Remain"
  Pillar: L'Invitation
  Goal: Last-minute June bookings. Quiet urgency. Zero pressure.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

— SLIDE 1 —
Visual: The atelier in late afternoon — the gold hour.
        Light slanting through the windows. An empty chair.
        Still. Beautiful. Waiting.

Text on screen:
  "June is nearly full."

Placement: Centred. One line. White serif. Long pause before it appears.

─

— SLIDE 2 —
Visual: A slow zoom into the appointment calendar — not a screenshot,
        but a close-up of the physical booking card or a beautifully
        designed digital version. Clean. Minimal. A few lines visible.
        Or: simply the atelier chair again, from a different angle.

Text on screen:
  "A few openings remain this week.
   For the Signature Cut.
   For beachwaves, tailor-made.
   For the brushing you've been meaning to book."

Placement: Left-aligned. Staggered lines. Unhurried.

─

— SLIDE 3 —
Visual: The Maison BU atelier exterior — the historic façade in
        summer evening light. Or: a final close-up of finished hair
        in golden light, the guest stepping toward the door.

Text on screen:
  "The atelier is ready.
   We would be glad to receive you."

Link sticker: maisonbu.be/book (placed cleanly beneath the text)
Placement: Centred. Warm. Never pushy.
